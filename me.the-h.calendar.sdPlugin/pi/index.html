1 shows the meeting on now or up next, 2 the one after it, and so on. Put keys set to 1, 2 and 3 side by side to see your next three meetings. On a dial this is where turning starts.

All buttons

Use your calendar's private iCal (.ics) address. In Google Calendar it's under Settings → your calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Anyone with that address can read your calendar, so it's stored only in deckd's plugin data on this computer, in plain text.

Meetings you declined are hidden. They're matched by your email, which defaults to the calendar's name (for Google, its owner's address). All-day events are not shown.

Press the key to join the meeting when it has a link and starts within 15 minutes; otherwise a press refreshes. On a dial, turn to step through the coming meetings, push to join the one shown and tap the strip to refresh.
