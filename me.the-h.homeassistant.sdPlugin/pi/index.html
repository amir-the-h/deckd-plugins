All buttons

Press does what suits the entity: toggles lights, switches, fans and covers, runs scripts, activates scenes, presses buttons, locks or unlocks, plays or pauses media, starts or docks a vacuum. On a dial, turning adjusts brightness, fan speed, volume, target temperature, cover position or a number. Show attribute displays one of the entity's attributes instead of its state.

Press to start cleaning, or pause and resume while it cleans; hold for a second to stop it (leave the slot's gestures unset so the plugin can time the hold). On a dial, turn to pick a room from the vacuum's map, then press to clean just that room. Rooms are read from the vacuum's rooms attribute; the room-cleaning call is found automatically (Dreame, Roborock, Xiaomi) or set by Room service and Room data, where "$room" stands for the room's id.

Pressing calls the service on the entity (if set) with the data. The key shows the state of "Show state of", or of the entity, when set.

Create the token in Home Assistant under your profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens. It is stored in plain text in deckd's plugin data, readable only by you.
